# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are prospective research supervisors, research collaborators, and graduate-program reviewers evaluating Chenggang Zhang's academic work. They need to understand his research direction, inspect credible evidence, and decide whether to make contact.

## Product Purpose

This single-page academic homepage presents Chenggang Zhang's research profile, publications, projects, research experience, education, awards, and contact details. Success means a visitor can quickly identify his focus in intelligent vehicles and sustainable mobility, verify the work in depth, and contact him by email.

## Positioning

The site connects intelligent-vehicle perception, motion prediction, and predictive energy management in one evidence-led engineering profile, with complete publications and project results visible directly on the homepage.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically arrive from an application, paper, research introduction, or direct link. They scan the first viewport, then inspect publications, projects, laboratories, education, and awards before using email or GitHub. The site is a static GitHub Pages deployment at `chenggangzhang.com`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plain HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript; no build step, server, framework, or third-party API.
- All seven current publications remain fully expanded on the homepage with their authors, venues, status, DOI, metadata, preview images, and links; manuscripts in preparation are shown in a clearly separate, non-counted group.
- All four projects, three research experiences, two schools, news items, award text, and real contact links remain intact.
- Certificate files remain in `assets/certificates/`; the homepage may present a static curated subset with an accessible user-controlled expansion for the rest.
- No CV, Download CV, Resume, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, invented claim, invented metric, or placeholder link may be added.
- The page must remain usable without JavaScript and responsive from 360px through 1440px.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Chenggang Zhang.
- Professional, restrained, modern international academic voice with a tangible vehicle-engineering character.
- Preserve the established deep-green identity; avoid neon technology styling, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, or persistent motion.
- Use the existing local Source Sans 3 and local imagery, logos, figures, certificates, and portrait.

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete factual page copy in `index.html`.
- Publication figures and PDFs in `assets/publications/`.
- Institution logos and portrait in `assets/`.
- Certificate originals and previews in `assets/certificates/library/`.
- Existing email, GitHub, DOI, publication, certificate, and internal-anchor links.
- No additional social profiles or CV entry are approved for display.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before decoration: publications and projects carry the argument.
2. Complete, direct access: core academic work is never hidden, paginated, or reduced to titles.
3. Engineering clarity: structure, labels, and quantitative results stay precise and verifiable.
4. Quiet confidence: visual polish supports credibility without spectacle or continuous motion.
5. Contact follows understanding: email and GitHub appear after the visitor has enough proof to act.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic landmarks, skip navigation, keyboard operation, visible focus, accurate alternative text, reduced-motion support, usable 44px touch targets, and layouts without horizontal overflow at supported viewport widths.
